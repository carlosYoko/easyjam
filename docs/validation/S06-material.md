### New annotated candidate: GAPS — rights clarification required

Expanded search query: `guitar dataset full songs aligned score creative commons audio` (public search HTML, no API credentials). Primary sources inspected 2026-10-31:

- [Publisher site](https://aim-qmul.github.io/GAPS/): 300 real classical-guitar performances, score-aligned MIDI, MusicXML, downbeats and video links. The demo exposes separate audio, MIDI, XML and sync-point files, making this a substantially better reference candidate than an untimed chord sheet.
- [Original Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/13962272) and [current v2 record](https://zenodo.org/records/17152440): CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 metadata; archives are explicitly `no_audio`. Current record recommends the author's [Hugging Face release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xavriley/GAPS), whose [README](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xavriley/GAPS/raw/main/README.md) says v1.1 includes audio and fixes case-sensitive identifiers, but declares **MIT**. The publisher site separately imposes individual/group-only noncommercial research use, no redistribution without written permission and potentially updated terms. These scopes are not reconciled; do not select the least restrictive label.
- [Paper §3.1, §3.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.08653v1): scores came from ClassClef; audio/video were downloaded from YouTube. Initial model-based alignment was manually checked at downbeats by experienced musicians, score/performance disagreements corrected where possible, and fine alignment/model-agreement filtering applied. This is valuable external reference provenance, not an error-free hand-labelled onset/offset oracle. The authors explicitly could not trace the ClassClef data's provenance and did not verify the tablature against each performer's string/fret choices.

**Disposition:** promising alternative for complete guitar/reference pairs, but not cleared. Publisher-hosted audio is not evidence that the publisher can grant all composition, recording and score rights. A mirror or dataset copy does not automatically establish authorized independent audio under S06. No GAPS audio/MIDI archive was downloaded, no YouTube extraction performed and no author contacted. Clarify a specific piece/performance and allowed audio/reference analysis, derivatives and local retention before adopting it. Written clarification would be a concrete route, unlike repeatedly searching for a Code Monkey timed chart. **The user explicitly declined third-party contact in this continuation; no inquiry was published and this route is unavailable under current authorization.**

Additional public search query: `guitar transcription dataset full songs GOAT license`. The [GOAT publisher README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JackJamesLoth/GOAT-Dataset/main/README.md) confirms real electric-guitar recordings with paired tablature/MIDI, but directs users to request full-dataset access at Zenodo.
